# tv-hero

**A calm home screen and family controls for Android TV, run from your phone.** No ads, no feeds, no accounts, no tracking.

[Download the latest release](https://github.com/ajitpal/tv-hero-releases/releases/latest){.button} [How to install](install.html){.button .ghost}

## What it does

- **A clean Home.** A dock of your apps, a clock and greeting, and a line about whatever you're on: what you watched there lately, or what a button does.
- **Profiles for everyone.** Each profile sees only its own apps plus the Apps for Everyone, with a daily limit and bedtime. Guest mode, with its own allowance.
- **Time's up means the TV stops.** Playback pauses and the app closes. Kids can ask for more time (it appears on your phone) or **earn it** by answering questions in Maths, English, General knowledge, Hindi and Puzzles.
- **From your phone.** Who's watching, give or take time, end today's time now, approve apps and requests, pause the TV, sleep timer, night volume, a remote and a game controller.
- **Tools on the big screen.** Timer, Stopwatch and Workout (HIIT presets, your own workouts) with beeps, progress rings and a small timer in the corner over other apps.
- **A watch screensaver.** An analogue watch and the date on a gradient that follows the time of day.
- **Updates from here.** The phone app checks these releases, updates itself and installs the TV app over your home Wi-Fi, after checking every file's checksum and signature.

## Security, in short

Phone and TV talk over **mutual TLS with pinned keys**. Pairing compares a six-digit code on both screens and needs **Allow on the TV**. Control over the internet is **off by default**. Every phone can be seen and removed. Nothing that loosens a rule works without the parent password. [More](security.html)

## Privacy

tv-hero has no accounts, no analytics and no ads. What was watched stays on the TV. The only outside connection is the phone app's update check to GitHub, which you can switch off. [More](privacy.html)
