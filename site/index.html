For parents

Screen time that actually holds,
without the arguments.

Rules live on the TV, so they work even when your phone is in another room. Loosening a rule always needs your password; tightening never does.

A profile for every child

Each sees only their own apps plus the ones for everyone, with their own daily limit and bedtime. Up to ten profiles, plus Guest.

Time's up means stop

The video pauses, the app closes and nothing opens behind the screen. The only ways on: ask you, earn time, or your PIN.

Bedtime

A good-night screen, then standby a minute later. One more hour tonight? Allow it from your phone.

Apps you approve

Anything newly installed waits for your OK. Settings and the Play Store are for parents only.

Time Today, from your phone

Give 15 more minutes, take some away, or end today's TV now. Kids' requests appear under Needs You.

Fair profile switching

One free switch a day; after that, kids ask you. Moving to a profile without limits always needs a parent.

Pause the whole TV

Dinner time? One tap stops everything until you resume it. A sleep timer and a night volume cap too.

Only real watching counts

Time is counted only while an app is open: not Home, the screensaver, Settings or the built-in tools.

For kids

Out of time?
Earn some more.

When the day's TV is used up, kids can press Earn Time and answer questions on the big screen. Every right answer adds minutes, up to a daily maximum you choose. A wrong answer costs nothing, but three in a row means a short break, so guessing doesn't pay.

Questions

Which TVs does it work on?

Android TV and Google TV from Android 9 on. It was built on a Xiaomi Mi TV 4A Pro and is being tested on more. On Google TV devices that insist on their own Home screen, tv-hero runs as a guard: profiles, limits and lock screens still work.

Do I need the phone all the time?

No. The rules live on the TV and work without the phone. A Parent Menu on the TV (with your PIN) does the essentials too.

Can kids get around it?

Settings and the Play Store are parent-only in kid profiles, new apps wait for approval, and switching to a profile without limits needs a parent. Someone with physical access to Settings in the Parent profile could switch the guard off; that's why Parent is behind a PIN.

What does it collect?

Nothing. There are no accounts, analytics or ads. The only outside connection is the phone app's update check to GitHub, which you can switch off.

Is it free?

Yes, free and open source under the MIT licence.
