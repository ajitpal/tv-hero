01 · On the TV

Home

Calm, and awake to your family.

No ads, no autoplay rows, no feeds. A clock, a greeting, your apps, and a line about what you're on: what you watched there lately, or what a button does.

Capabilities
  • Each child sees only their apps
  • Apps for Everyone, chosen by you
  • Recent titles under each app
  • Light enough for a 1 GB TV
  • Remote-friendly, predictable focus
  • Settings and Play Store for parents
02 · For kids

Earn Time

Out of time? Earn some more.

When the day's TV is used up, kids press Earn Time and answer questions on the big screen. Each right answer adds minutes, up to a daily maximum you choose. A wrong answer costs nothing, but three in a row means a short break, so guessing doesn't pay.

  • Easy, Medium and Hard
  • Set for each child
  • Works offline
  • Nothing tracked
  • Cheers for right answers
  • Kind words for wrong ones

04 · Time & bedtime

Rules that hold

Time's up means stop.

The video pauses, the app closes and nothing opens behind the screen. The only ways on: ask you, earn time, or your PIN. At bedtime, a good-night screen, then standby.

  • Daily limit per child
  • Bedtime per child
  • One free profile switch
  • Then kids ask you
  • Only real watching counts
  • Password to loosen, never to tighten
05 · Tools & screensaver

Tools

The living room, put to work.

Timer, Stopwatch and Workout with big digits and progress rings for the whole room. Start a Tabata, open YouTube, and a small timer stays in the corner.

  • HIIT presets like Tabata
  • 3-2-1 beeps, colour per phase
  • Corner timer over any app
  • Start them from the phone
  • A quiet watch screensaver
  • Never counted as screen time

06 · Set up

A new TV in about two minutes.
No computer, no cables.

1

Install the phone app

Download tv-hero for your phone from the latest release.

2

Tap Set Up a New TV

Switch on TV debugging (the app shows you where). The phone finds the TV, you press Allow, and it installs and sets up tv-hero.

3

Pair and add your family

Compare a six-digit code on both screens, press Allow on the TV, and create profiles.

Questions
Which TVs does it work on?

Android TV and Google TV from Android 9 on. It was built on a Xiaomi Mi TV 4A Pro and is being tested on more. On Google TV devices that insist on their own Home screen, tv-hero runs as a guard: profiles, limits and lock screens still work.

Do I need the phone all the time?

No. The rules live on the TV and work without the phone. A Parent Menu on the TV, behind your PIN, does the essentials too.

Can kids get around it?

Settings and the Play Store are parent-only in kid profiles, new apps wait for approval, and switching to a profile without limits needs a parent. Someone with access to Settings in the Parent profile could switch the guard off, which is why Parent is behind a PIN.

What does it collect?

Nothing. No accounts, analytics or ads. The only outside connection is the phone app's update check to GitHub, which you can switch off.

Is it free?

Yes. Free and open source under the MIT licence.
