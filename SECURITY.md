# Security policy

tv-hero controls a family TV, so security reports are very welcome.

**Please don't open a public issue for a vulnerability.** Report it privately with GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button, on this repository's *Security* tab. Include the version (Settings → About on the phone), your TV model and Android version, and the steps.

You'll get an answer within a few days. Fixes ship as a new release, and the phone app offers the update within a day.

How tv-hero protects a family, and its known limits: [docs/SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md).
